It creates no public catalogue sources and makes no stream, EPG or media-rights claims. Machine-readable records and the primary-source register are in the accompanying JSON.

The Basque decisions use the project’s substantive-authority rule: the three historical territories have protected foral powers and separate representative organs controlling their executives and territorial budgets. Their location below an autonomous community is not an automatic exclusion under the user’s autonomous special-status scope. Formal law rank remains reserved to the Basque Parliament; the JSON distinguishes that limit. [Basque Statute](https://boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-1979-30177&lang=es), [Law 27/1983, articles 6 and8](https://boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2012-5193).

Each organ is independently identified in primary institutional material: [Álava article 4 account](https://www.jjggalava.eus/funciones-generales), [Bizkaia NF 3/1987, articles 4–7](https://jjggbizkaia.eus/documents/8778164/0/A02-I%2B2023%2Bnoviem%2B(1).pdf/2b516b33-0d93-fede-d799-8b12850876bb?t=1751881013374), [Gipuzkoa NF 6/2005, articles 4–6](https://egoitza.gipuzkoa.eus/ogasuna/normativa/docs/0001202c.pdf). These are core-authority findings, not a claim that every 2026 amendment was reconciled.

Norway’s Sámi Parliament has elected representation, meaningful consultation and delegated funding/regulation powers. Sweden’s elected Sametinget is also a special agency with cultural, consultation and administrative responsibilities. Neither establishes the autonomous territorial legislative government within this inventory’s scope. [Norwegian Sámi Act](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/documents/the-sami-act-/id449701/), [Swedish current Sametingslag, through SFS 2026:136](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/sametingslag-19921433_sfs-1992-1433/).

Longyearbyen is a municipal-like government covering its planning area, not a Svalbard territorial parliament. Local regulation and budgeting remain acknowledged. [Government Svalbard white paper2.6.3](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/documents/meld.-st.-26-20232024/id3041130/?ch=2).

Luxembourg’s Conseil d’État has a genuine suspensive legislative role: refusal of the second-vote waiver delays enactment. It is nevertheless a constitutional advisory institution in a unicameral system rather than an additional chamber. [Constitution78(4),95](https://conseil-etat.public.lu/fr/legislation/extraits_constitution.html), [official government explanation](https://gouvernement.lu/fr/systeme-politique/conseil-etat.html).

For Timor-Leste, use the operative 2026 RAEOA framework rather than only the old 2015 organisation rules. The Authority and Consultative Council remain supervised administrative organs. Ataúro’s General Council has administrative oversight and budget approval subject to ministerial homologation; its local regulatory proposals go to ministers. [RAEOA DL 1/2026](https://www.mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2026/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_4_C.pdf), [Ataúro DL 82/2023, articles 5–8](https://mj.gov.tl/jornal/public/docs/2023/serie_1/SERIE_I_NO_44_B.pdf).

Fernando de Noronha’s elected District Council scrutinises administration and approves accounts, but legislative proposals and budget enactment use Pernambuco’s state process. These meaningful powers do not establish a regional legislature. [Organic Law 11.304/1995, articles 16,36–37,51](https://www.noronha.pe.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/LEI-No-11.304-DE-28-DE-DEZEMBRO-DE-1995.pdf), [current institutional class](https://www.noronha.pe.gov.br/transparencia/informacoes-institucionais/).

All 11 named organs now have explicit dispositions; there are no evidence holds in this file. The JSON preserves version/currentness limits and suggested IDs. This file does not itself certify all 71 countries or every statutory amendment.
