## Main findings

- Umbrella legislature counts cannot substitute for named chambers: US56umbrellas represent108subnational chambers; Bermuda/Tynwald need chamber children.
- Philippine82provincialboards+Bangsamoro match provincial scope, but independent-city exclusion requires explicit resolution.
- Korean current successor is corroborated;16active regional bodies plus2historical records.
- SriLanka9statutory councils must not be presented as9currently sitting elected bodies.
- Swiss26cantons cover the regional universe, with two direct-popular Landsgemeinden needing separate organ identities.
- Indigenous/tribal and second-level special legislative organs are scope questions, not proven absent or automatically municipal.
