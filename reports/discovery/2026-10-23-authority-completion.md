### Czech Republic — czech-higher-territorial-assemblies (14 bodies)

Constitution Article 104 distinguishes higher territorial self-government and gives its assemblies generally binding ordinances. The Regions Act 129/2000 separates independent regional legislation from delegated state administration and reserves ordinances and budget to the elected assembly. These regional assemblies meet the substantive regional-ordinance rule. Prague qualifies in its combined regional capacity under Act 131/2000, not every municipal council.

The ministry’s English Regions Act is a historical translation, read for the independent/delegated distinction with the constitutional and governmental explanations; not a certification of every current procedural amendment.
