# Papuan Majelis Rakyat: six institutional scope decisions

Reviewed **24 October 2026**. Inventory and authority only. This closes the specific Indonesia regional-report caveat about MRP; it does not revisit the separately resolved national MPR/DPD distinction or validate streams, metadata, EPG or rights.

**All six MRPs are outside the default catalogue scope.** Keep them as named, legally significant cultural and Indigenous-rights consent institutions alongside the six existing provincial DPRP legislatures. Add no legislative chambers or provincial jurisdictions from this decision.

## Why this is a resolved exclusion

The current [UU 2/2021](https://mrp.papuabaratdayaprov.go.id/storage//documents/UU%20Nomor%202%20Tahun%202021_1709527639_compressed.pdf), replacing Articles 1, 5, 7 and 20 of the special-autonomy law, expressly separates provincial government/DPRP from MRP's cultural representation. It places MRP and DPRP in each provincial capital. DPRP adopts provincial legislation and the APBD with the governor and exercises general supervision; MRP gives consideration and consent in the specified Perdasus and Indigenous-rights processes. The 2021 replacement was directly read from the official institution's linked statutory PDF, including pages 4, 7–8 and 10–13.

MRP consent has legal consequences. [PP 54/2004](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp54-2004.pdf), Article 38, sets the Perdasus consideration/consent process and deemed-consent period, followed by provincial government/DPRP enactment. Articles 42–44 provide rights-related information and reconsideration requests and submission of MRP's institutional budget to DPRP. Articles 45/54 concern its own rules/decisions; Article 74 anticipates MRP in successor provinces. These powers do not supply the general territorial legislative and budget role needed for an additional catalogue legislature. This project judgement does not dismiss MRP as merely voluntary advice or deny its statutory authority.

The [2008 amendment](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/37408/PP%2064%20Tahun%202008.pdf) adjusts financial provisions. [PP 106/2021](https://mrp.papuatengahprov.go.id/storage/documents/PP%20Nomor%20106%20Tahun%202021_1709527717.pdf), Article 103, preserves earlier non-conflicting provisions. Its [2025 amendment](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp-no-46-tahun-2025.pdf) was also checked: it alters Articles 86 and 90 on development-steering governance, not MRP's legislative allocation.

MRP means **Majelis Rakyat Papua** here. National **Majelis Permusyawaratan Rakyat (MPR)** is a different institution. Separate legal personalities and shared provincial territory are both retained; MRP is not substituted for DPRP or treated as a second chamber merely because it holds plenaries or participates in the Perdasus process.

## What was confirmed and what was not

The common statutory authority allocation is confirmed and each of the six institutions has named own/provincial corroboration. The JSON records contain permanent review IDs, existing jurisdiction relationships, article locators, source methods and dated limits. No authority hold remains in this bounded class.

The Papua Pegunungan root/article returned empty text in the direct reader. Its official search-index content supplies the named identity and January 2026 plenary corroboration; full direct-page inspection is not claimed. The [South Papua appointment report](https://papuaselatan.go.id/berita/gubernur-apolo-lantik-dua-wakil-ketua-majelis-rakyat-papua-selatan) is dated 31 July 2025. Neither a dated appointment nor an active website certifies all current officeholders or continuous elected sitting.

The [Central Papua powers page](https://mrp.papuatengahprov.go.id/halaman/tugas-dan-kewenangan) still includes superseded pre-2021 MPR regional-delegate language. The current amending Act takes precedence. This page-version issue was identified rather than copied into the scope decision. The [Southwest Papua chair interview](https://mrp.papuabaratdayaprov.go.id/news/alfons-kambu-mrp-itu-lembaga-culture-bukan-lembaga-legislatif) is an own-site press republication, useful institutional corroboration rather than a statutory source.

Some national legal PDF endpoints timed out. The successful official MRP statutory downloads are the observed links from their document viewers; the law was not inferred absent from a failed request. PP 54/2004, PP 64/2008, PP 106/2021 and PP 46/2025 were directly read with their version relationships retained. This is a bounded scope review, not a certification of every provincial implementing amendment or current electoral roster.

## Inventory integration

Retain six `outside_default_scope` records and their existing province/DPRP relationships. Preserve each name so later researchers do not rediscover the same authority question. Reopen only on a specific enacted operative grant changing that allocation or an explicit scope change. No below-tier Indigenous/local-government census or stream publication follows from this review.
