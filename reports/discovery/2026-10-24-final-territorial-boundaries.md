# Remaining territorial boundaries and chamber corrections

Reviewed24October2026 under the agreed first-level/autonomous special-status scope. This is an inventory/authority pass; no service publication follows automatically.

## Named scope decisions

- **Conseil de Paris** — outside scope. Municipal/departmental hybrid below the regional tier. CGCT L2512-1 combines commune and department competences, not an autonomous territorial legislature. A special-status label does not supply a substantive autonomous legislative grant. [Primary source1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000034115674/2025-04-20)
- **Conseil de la Métropole de Lyon** — outside scope. Special municipal/departmental territorial collectivity below the regional tier. CGCT L3611-1–3 gives departmental/metropolitan administration; no separate autonomous regional lawmaking grant established. Keep distinct from the city municipal council. [Primary source1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070633/LEGISCTA000028528959/2026-05-09/)
- **Conseil de la Collectivité européenne d’Alsace** — outside scope. Departmental grouping with additional cross-border/language/transport functions, not a separate autonomous regional legislature on the operative law reviewed. The April2026 parliamentary proposal is not treated as an enacted change. Preserve future institutional reform as a reopening trigger. [Primary source1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000038872957/2026-06-08) · [Primary source2](https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/7929497) · [Primary source3](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/presse/gouvernement-lance-mission-bilan-creation-collectivite-europeenne-dalsace-consequences-dune)
- **Comisión de Desarrollo de Isla de Pascua (CODEIPA)** — outside scope. Law19253 article67 specifies land allocation/proposals, development projects and environmental/cultural administration; this is not a territorial legislature. Constitutional article126bis and transitional22 contemplate a special statute, not an already-created parliament. The official February2026 consultation is proposal evidence, not enactment. [Primary source1](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=30620&idParte=&idVersion=2025-09-29) · [Primary source2](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=242302) · [Primary source3](https://dppisladepascua.dpp.gob.cl/2026/02/02/con-amplia-participacion-del-pueblo-rapa-nui-finalizo-la-etapa-de-votacion-de-la-consulta-indigena-en-rapa-nui/)
