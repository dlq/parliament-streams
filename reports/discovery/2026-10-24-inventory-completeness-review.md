# Inventory Completeness — 24 October 2026

> Snapshot note: this report precedes the final scope decisions and operational integration. Use the [current reconciliation](2026-10-24-inventory-reconciliation.md) and [institution register](../../data/discovery/institutions.json) for the applied decisions and remaining legal holds.

**A complete list of all national and subnational legislative bodies has not been established.** All 71 baseline countries have a recorded country research pass. That is a search-coverage statement, not an institutional-census guarantee.

The regional/associated-territory ledger contains 1,122 entries: 652 eligible, 458 excluded, ten specific authority holds and two superseded institutions. Whole legislatures and individual chambers are not yet consistently separated. `inventory_complete` remains false. The 652 eligible entries are not a national-plus-regional chamber census.

The maintained 25/46 country split matches the EIU 2024 index published in 2025. It has not been reconciled with the actual 2025 edition. [Public explanation](../../docs/catalogue-scope.md#research-priorities-and-democracy-tiers).

## Work needed to establish completeness

1. Reconcile a named national parliament/chamber checklist for every baseline country with primary institutional directories.
2. Reconcile first-level state/provincial/devolved/autonomous legislatures against country-specific primary lists, retaining explicit no-regional-legislature scope evidence.
3. Split bicameral institutions into chamber records or provide a complete chamber crosswalk, while deduplicating shared services.
4. Record every associated/special-status territory and separately scoped Indigenous self-government class, with explicit included/excluded/authority-hold decisions.
5. Resolve the ten specific legal-authority holds; preserve the two superseded institutions and their successor mappings.
6. Record currentness/as-of dates and counts at each inventory grain. Refresh the EIU edition through a separately documented country-list comparison.
