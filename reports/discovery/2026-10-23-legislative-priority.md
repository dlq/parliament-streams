### Guadeloupe — France

Guadeloupe has exercised Article 73 legislative habilitations, but the 2026 National Assembly report says the 2022 energy-renewal request still lacked legislative follow-through. The 2015 grant and a published request cannot certify a present mandate. Preserve it as a specific current-grant evidence hold rather than an ordinary-council exclusion or unconditional eligibility.

**Evidence needed:** Locate a post-report enacted habilitation or still-operative extension/other substantive legislative mandate, with commencement and expiry dates. Historical proceedings during a documented grant may be assessed separately; do not promote a current mandate from the 2022 request.
