# Panama territorial authority holds — 24 October 2026

Three exact candidates received a fresh primary-source search. **All three remain specific authority evidence holds.** Their distinct identities are better established; none can responsibly receive legislative clearance or an exclusion based on the missing instruments.

| Existing ID | What is now confirmed | Remaining authority gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `panama--naso-tjer-di` | Traditional General Council is distinct from the King, the people’s assembly and municipal council. A 180-article Charter draft was validated in 2023. | Recover operative Charter/adoption, any sanctioning decree and commencement; determine which organ makes binding territorial rules and controls finance/executive authority. |
| `panama--bugle-people` | Recognised separately from the joint Ngäbe–Buglé comarca congress. It is not an alias to merge automatically. | Recover the approved Buglé-specific customary/internal instrument and show autonomous territorial jurisdiction, binding rules and financial/executive accountability. |
| `panama--guna-dagargunyala` | Separately recognised ancestral-territory Congress, also called Tagarkunyala, linked to Púcuro/Paya in Darién. | Recover its own operative customary rules and accountability framework; establish regional authority separately from community sagladuras. |

The enacted [Executive Decree 4 of 20 January 2025](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/storage/gacetas/2025/01/30204/110126.pdf), published in Gazette 30204 on 24 January, names all three separately in CONDIPI. This is newer and more precise official recognition than the old consultation matrix, but membership in the national policy body is not a territorial legislative grant. [COONAPIP’s own 2018 statute, articles 7–8](https://coonapipanama.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Estatuto-junio-2018.pdf) independently distinguishes their representative organs.

## Naso: distinguish the organs and the stages of adoption

[Law 188, articles 9–14](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/29170_A/GacetaNo_29170a_20201207.pdf) recognises the traditional Council and the separate people’s assembly while reserving their detailed powers to the Organic Charter. The [official legal register](https://infojuridica.procuraduria-admon.gob.pa/t_normas_print.php?numsec=55500) records commencement of the law on the day after promulgation, 8 December 2020. Its blank later-reference table is an indexed May 2026 snapshot, not a certificate that no Charter has since been enacted.

The [project participant’s account](https://www.fscindigenousfoundation.org/es/tag/comarca-naso-tjer-di/) confirms the Council validated a draft on 2 May 2023. [Concrete drafting slides](https://alt-perubolivia.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/4.1-PPT-Paisajes-culturales-NASO.FINAL-compres.pdf), pages 28–29, outline proposed environmental rules, but do not establish full customary commencement or financial/executive allocation. The [7 July 2025 ministry account](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/viceministra-de-asuntos-indigenas-y-autoridades-naso-abordan-temas-de-interes-para-esa-comarca/) and [Memoria 2025, page 47](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Memoria-2025-FINAL-BAJA-1.pdf) document the sanctioning process and goal. Neither proves the position in late 2026. Current [April 2026 boundary work](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/se-realiza-mesa-de-trabajo-que-busca-establecer-limites-y-demarcacion-de-comarcas/) also does not establish Charter enactment.

Targeted searches found no sanctioning decree or complete currently operative customary Charter. The official Gazette’s advanced-search page was inspected, but the form could not be submitted with available tools. Therefore the result is **not found in this pass**, not **not enacted**.

## Buglé: separate recognition is now established

The [ministry’s consultation record](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Analisis-Sociocultural-PN-L1157-marzo-2019-.pdf), Consulta 5, records distinct Buglé and joint-comarca delegations. The [joint comarca Charter](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/wp_repo/blogs.dir/cendoj/PUEBLOS_INDIGENAS/ngobe_bugle_arreglado.pdf) supplies territorial powers for that comarca’s Congress; it cannot serve as the independent Buglé Congress’s authority instrument.

A [report attributed to COONAPIP](https://es.slideshare.net/slideshow/coonapip-ii-foro-de-mujeres-bugle-elaborado-por-yanel-venado-jimenez-coinapip-panama/269493813) identifies an internal-regulation proposal. Its final adopted version and territorial application remain the retrieval target. The report’s platform attribution and date are not sufficient for clearance, or to conclude that it is still only a draft today.

## Dagargunyala: do not import Gunayala’s law

The [2020 Supreme Court judgment, Entrada 1490-18](https://repositoriodigital.organojudicial.gob.pa/bitstream/handle/001/231/Entrada%20N%C2%B0%201490-18.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=5) identifies the Púcuro/Paya claim and expressly leaves adjudication of the title proceeding outside its decision. It resolves the challenged park provision, not legislative status. The [Guna Congress’s 20 May 2026 notice](https://www.gunayala.org.pa/index_Gigga.htm) confirms a distinct Dagargunyala leadership. Gunayala’s Fundamental Law, budgets and general resolutions do not automatically apply to it.

Its own customary instrument or authentic adopted territorial rules can establish eligibility without a state Act. The missing evidence is their operative scope, binding effect, current adoption and financial/executive controls. Two communities, ancestral collective title or a representative Congress alone do not decide that question.
