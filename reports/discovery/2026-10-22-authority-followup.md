## Authority decisions

### Region Skåne regionfullmäktige

**outside_default_scope**. The documented role fits the project's regional service/local-government exclusion. Taxation, elections and executive scrutiny do not alone establish the required legislative status. This is a catalogue decision, not a denial of local self-government or delegated regulation.

Reject sweden-skane for catalogue scope; retain the evidence, independently of HTTP 403.

Evidence:
- [opened official institutional page](https://www.skane.se/en/politics-and-democracy/how-region-skane-is-governed/): Elected regional council controls healthcare, transport, regional development, culture, budgets and regional tax.
- [opened official constitutional text](https://www.riksdagen.se/globalassets/03.-dokument-och-lagar/bestall-och-ladda-ner/other-languages/grundlagar-2023-engelsk-web.pdf): Instrument of Government chapters 8 and 14 distinguish Acts of the Riksdag, delegated provisions and elected local-authority decision making.
- [opened consolidated official statute (through SFS 2026:836)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2017-725.html): Local Government Act chapter 5 section 1 governs council goals, budget, tax, organisation and accountability; future commencement clauses must be considered separately.

Remaining: Reconsider only if specific legislative-status evidence or the project's scope changes.

### Grosser Rat des Kantons Bern

**eligible_legislature**. Cantonal legislature with substantive law-making, finance and oversight powers. The name Grand Council is not a reason to exclude it.

Authority gate passes; broadcast-service promotion remains separate.

Evidence:

Remaining: Resolve a current public session/archive landing page, identity, source conditions and accessibility. The indexed video test page is a lead, not a production-service validation.

### Nagasaki Prefectural Assembly

**authority_review_required**. There are real ordinance-making and executive-scrutiny powers. Calling these merely a service board would be inaccurate. The unresolved catalogue question is whether this constitutionally protected local legislative model qualifies under the agreed distinction from ordinary local councils; neither first-level status nor the English word assembly settles it.

Retain existing catalogue records; do not expand all 47 prefectures on the basis of the assembly name.

Evidence:
- [opened official assembly explanation](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/gikai/4020.html): Assembly adopts, amends and repeals prefectural ordinances, decides budgets, consents to important appointments and investigates executive administration.
- [opened official assembly basic ordinance](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/gikai/3001.html): Articles 2 and 3 describe the assembly's independent representative, decision-making, scrutiny and policy roles.
- [official constitutional translation surfaced in search](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/download/174/09/s21Ak000010101en3.0.pdf): Constitution article 94 protects local entities' administration and enactment of regulations within law.

Remaining: Compare the scope of autonomous prefectural ordinances and sanctions with municipal powers under the current Local Autonomy Act, then record a consistent scope decision. No removal or eligibility assertion is made in this pass.

### Twelve Dutch provincial councils

**authority_review_required**. Provincial ordinance authority includes an autonomous component; it must not be described as exclusively delegated national administration. A title-based exclusion is as unsupported as automatic inclusion.

Retain all twelve as held; do not apply this assessment to Aruba, Curaçao, Sint Maarten or Caribbean island councils.

Evidence:
- [opened official English constitutional translation, 2018 edition](https://www.government.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2019/02/28/the-constitution-of-the-kingdom-of-the-netherlands/WEB_119406_Grondwet_Koninkrijk_ENG.pdf): Articles 124 and 127 establish provincial internal-affairs regulation and council enactment of ordinances, alongside municipal provisions; this is not a current consolidated statute check.
- [official government guidance surfaced in search](https://www.government.nl/themes/government-and-democracy/provinces/provincial-executive-members/the-tasks-of-provincial-executive-members): Provincial councils hold a separate provincial executive accountable.
- [direct access failed (429/robots); official search-index excerpts available](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005645/): Indexed Provinciewet articles 143 and 145 describe council ordinances, including those judged necessary in provincial interests. Do not treat a dated indexed consolidation as verified current law.

Remaining: Read a current accessible official consolidation and compare autonomous rule-making with the local-government exclusion. The twelve province rows remain held; constituent-country parliaments retain separate decisions.
