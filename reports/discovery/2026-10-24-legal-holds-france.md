## Why close these holds?

A hold should identify an unresolved operative instrument, not demand indefinite proof against every hypothetical future delegation. The reviewed ordinary framework requires a separate subject-specific authorization. Guadeloupe’s former mandate has an explicit expiry; the 2026 paragraph 3 practice review lists successful use by Guadeloupe/Martinique and names unsuccessful Guyane requests. Current paragraph 2 adaptation is independently conditional underLO 7411-6. Apply default scope with an exact reopening test; do not infer an exhaustive denial of every adaptation grant from that practice report.

Do not exclude merely because rules are delegated, bounded or called regulations. The accepted Czech/Croatian/Japanese/provincial cases have affirmative substantive regional norm-making grants under their ordinary framework. These three records were held specifically for an unproved separate Article 73 grant; a general route for requesting one is not itself that affirmative grant. This decision does not deny ordinary regulatory, budget or representative powers.

Guadeloupe’s former energy grant expired **27 June 2021** according to its own gazetted renewal request. The National Assembly’s 10 June 2026 report confirms that request had no legislative follow-through. Guyane/Mayotte share the conditional 2026 framework; the actual 30 June 2026 grant is expressly for Martinique. Mayotte’s national-government social ordinance is a different constitutional mechanism.

## Reopening test

Reopen on an actual body-specific operative territorial legislative grant, including subject/date/expiry/renewal. A proposal, generic enabling procedure, old rule still in force, national-government ordinance or renamed assembly alone is insufficient.
