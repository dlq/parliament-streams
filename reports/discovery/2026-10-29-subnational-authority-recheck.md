# Subnational authority evidence recheck — 29 October 2026

## Decision and scope

Retain the three Basque foral assemblies (Álava/Araba, Bizkaia and Gipuzkoa),
following the project owner's explicit acceptance pending a substantive challenge.
This does not admit ordinary Spanish provincial diputaciones, relax the municipal
exclusion, or confer formal law rank on foral norms. The 17 autonomous-community
parliaments remain the clear Spanish regional-legislature class.

The other subnational inclusions must continue to have substantive territorial
lawmaking evidence. The [scope guide](../../docs/catalogue-scope.md#evidence-required-for-a-subnational-decision)
now makes the required distinctions and legal-evidence checklist explicit.
Existing ordinance-based decisions remain project scope decisions; describing
all of them as state-style statutory parliaments would overstate the evidence.

## Full-register checks

At commit `9d502338fc6295e87c98dcc221aadfde4fa02a6c`, the operational register has
**835 eligible subnational identity records in 41 country groupings**, alongside
105 eligible national records. These are identities at different grains, not 835
separate territorial legislatures. There are 142 added chamber/legislative-organ records using the shared
framework explanation: 134 constituent chambers, six Indian sibling upper
chambers and two Swiss direct-democracy organs.

A programmatic check of every eligible subnational record found:

- All 835 have a decision explanation, primary-source URLs and report provenance.
- None retains a non-empty list of missing authority instruments.
- All 142 added chamber/organ records resolve to eligible institutional anchors,
  preserving their relationship types: 134 parent-legislature links, six sibling
  upper-chamber links and two distinct same-canton direct-democracy organs.
  The last eight must not be misrepresented as children of those anchors.
- IDs are unique; all 835 have the same eligible status in the generated public
  inventory. No source/public status drift was found.

These are evidence-presence and relationship checks. They are **not independent
legal validation of every citation**, proof that each text is current, or fresh
proof that an assembly is currently sitting. The eight existing instrument-specific
legal holds remain outside the eligible queue. No institution or viewing source
is added or removed by this report.

## Source-access limits and remaining legal refresh

Colombia's existing 32 departmental-assembly decisions cite Constitution
Articles 299–300 and Act 2200/2022. In this pass the government legal-text/PDF
routes returned 403/502; another official PDF exceeded the reader's size limit.
Indexed official excerpts corroborated ordinance powers, but the full operative
text was not freshly read. Retain the earlier evidence and record a retrieval
follow-up, rather than calling this a fresh confirmation or changing eligibility.

The other 528 records retain their earlier authority evidence. This report does
not certify that every current constitution, autonomy statute, treaty, habilitation
and individual chamber identity has been reopened. Priority follow-up is:

1. Complete the Colombian current-text retrieval and verify the substantive
   ordinance/administrative-act distinction.
2. Refresh individual special-status grants and their limits: Martinique's
   habilitation/expiry, Mount Athos's exact legislative organ and ratification,
   Papuan DPR/MRP distinctions, Indian autonomous councils and Panamanian comarca
   congresses. Do not replace these with a generic regional-government analogy.
3. Check current consolidations where this pass used older explanatory texts
   (notably Poland, Japan and Suriname), and retrieve the Dutch statute without
   treating rate limiting as an authority decision.
4. Continue the remaining existing frameworks and exact chamber identities,
   retaining dated source-specific findings. Reopen a classification when the
   operative provision does not substantiate it; do not automatically grandfather
   bodies merely because a prior report marked them eligible.
