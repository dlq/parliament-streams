# Indian autonomous-legislature scope resolution —24October 2026

All ten named Sixth Schedule scope questions are resolved as **eligible autonomous special-status legislatures**. There are **no remaining authority-evidence holds in this ten-body set**. This is an inventory decision; it adds no catalogue sources or streams.

The decision applies the [maintained scope rule](../../docs/catalogue-scope.md) to substantive territorial legislation, finance and executive accountability. These bodies sit inside states, but the special-status rule does not require sovereign or first-level status. Ordinary municipal powers and a council title alone do not qualify a body.

## Shared constitutional basis

Article 244(2) and the Sixth Schedule establish autonomous territorial councils. Paragraph 3 covers land, non-reserved forest, inheritance, marriage/divorce and custom; Assam has extra grants in 3A/3B. Laws require assent and official publication. Paragraphs 7/8 provide funds, tax powers and CAG reporting to councils;13 requires discussion of relevant state estimates. Paragraphs 15/16 allow intervention. These are bounded legislatures, not sovereign states. [Official 2026 Constitution](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316 f74bb1c556c.pdf).

Assam has additional constitutional subjects and presidential reservation for concurrent-list measures. Meghalaya retains explicit state-law supremacy on conflict. Tripura and Mizoram have their own scheduled-subject state-law application provisions. The relevant limits are retained in the [machine-readable report](2026-10-24-scope-resolution-india.json), rather than presenting these as unrestricted state parliaments.

## Names, activity and evidence limits

- **Bodoland Territorial Council:** Council name retained as BTC; BTR names its territory. A 2020 political settlement is not treated as self-executing enlargement of every constitutional grant or council seat count.
- **Karbi Anglong Autonomous Council:** Keep KAAC distinct from the statutory Karbi Welfare Autonomous Council for Karbi communities outside its area. Do not equate civil-district subdivision with creation of another council.
- **North Cachar Hills Autonomous Council (Dima Hasao):** Preserve both legal and current display names and one institutional identity. No new institution is inferred from the district rename; 2026 customary-law Bill listing is not an enactment claim.
- **Khasi Hills Autonomous District Council:** The autonomous area crosses multiple civil districts and contains traditional Hima/Elaka units; do not create five parliaments from those district names or automatically promote every traditional council.
- **Jaintia Hills Autonomous District Council:** Keep it separate from KHADC and from East/West Jaintia Hills civil administrations. Historical auditor findings and 2025 state notices are dated structural corroboration, not a 2026 elected-member certification.
- **Garo Hills Autonomous District Council:** One autonomous council encompasses five civil districts. Its institutional existence in law is retained without asserting uninterrupted elected operation, a current term, or the current force of every historical listed Act.
- **Tripura Tribal Areas Autonomous District Council:** Preserve the historical 1979 statutory origin/1982 operation separately from Sixth Schedule operation from 1985. Proposed Tipra Territorial Council naming or expansion is not adopted without an enacted operative instrument.
- **Chakma Autonomous District Council:** Retain council-in-law independently of periods of Governor administration. The 24 August 2026 sitting report is dated confirmation, not certification of the executive or electoral situation on every subsequent date.
- **Lai Autonomous District Council:** Lai/Pawi and Mara/Lakher are separate successor identities; never map Pawi to Mara. Do not copy principal 2002 membership figures over later amendments or infer current operation solely from historical rules.
- **Mara Autonomous District Council:** Own-site 403 and unavailable 2002 PDF do not erase a clear constitutional grant. Current elected composition/activity remains unasserted. Avoid the apparent Pawi-to-Mara error in one historical CAG introductory snippet; current Constitution and state/own Lai account distinguish Lai/Pawi and Mara/Lakher.

Governor assent, presidential reservation, state grants or possible suspension do not remove institutional eligibility. They do mean that a council-in-law must be kept separate from its current electoral term, executive and actual sitting activity. This pass does not certify all October 2026 officeholders, continuous elected operation, or every amendment to historical conduct-of-business rules.

## One additional territorial instrument: FNTA

The broader currentness check identified **Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority**, covering six eastern districts within Nagaland. It is a separate statutory territory lead, **not an eleventh Sixth Schedule council** and not an ordinary village council.

The [official 15 September 2026 Gazette](https://govtpress.nagaland.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/15-September-GB-1_compressed.pdf), Part V, contains the revised Bill introduced on 1 September under notification AS/LEG-22/Bill/FNTA/2026/298. Rendered PDF pages 55, 56 and 59–64 were individually read: clauses 10–12 provide territorial lawmaking and a house resolution process, followed by state examination, Governor assent and publication. Clause 1(3) requires a commencement notification. This is substantive evidence of the revised proposal; the original March executive-only wording cannot settle the September institution's scope.

**Disposition: specific authority-evidence hold.** The exact missing instruments are the final assented Act, including enacted amendments to the lawmaking provisions, and its Gazette commencement/constitution notification. The state Acts directory did not supply them. The [30 September Gazette index](https://govtpress.nagaland.gov.in/egazette-30-september-2026/) supplies an official download, but its web-reader size limit and normal 45/120-second download timeouts prevented inspection. No inference that the Act does not exist is made. This narrowly defined hold preserves the additional candidate in the reconciled inventory while leaving the ten original Indian scope decisions resolved.
