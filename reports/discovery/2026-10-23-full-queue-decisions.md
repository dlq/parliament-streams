# Full-queue validation and legislative priorities

23 October 2026, first validation stage. Later local totals and additions are in the [second pass](2026-10-23-validation-second-pass.md); the counts below describe this earlier stage, not deployment.

**27 additional official viewing services:** 21 national and six regional/territorial, beyond the previously prepared Cook Islands and Guernsey additions. The catalogue now has 156 sources, including 72 national, 78 regional/territorial and six supranational records.

National catalogue coverage reaches 53 of the 71 maintained target countries; 18 gaps remain (two Tier1 and sixteen Tier2). Further chambers, committees and current live coverage remain open even in covered countries.

## What the scanning established

- Bounded public HTTP access triage completed 931 retained lead families: 70 national and 861 initially non-excluded regional entries.
- Normal Chromium player screening completed 318 regional routes with media clues. Signals are retained separately from proceedings/identity/rights decisions.
- The [legislative-priority register](2026-10-23-legislative-priority.md) now identifies 366 eligible bodies, 85 core candidates, 408 unresolved authority reviews and 262 documented exclusions.
- Excluded bodies retain historical research and media links but are removed from active publication validation. Failed players remain technical holds.

## Important evidence distinctions

- Jamaica: sampled Senate-labelled videos contained PSAs. Actual public-account and domestic-violence committee recordings support the addition; Senate playback is unvalidated.
- Indonesia: portal import dates are not meeting dates. Provider recordings include historical proceedings; the source is labelled an archive.
- Appenzell Ausserrhoden: the current constitutional version is effective 26 November 2023 through 31 December 2026; a 2027 version is not current.
- Wallonia expressly permits attributed, contextual reproduction of debates/recordings; its website-framing condition is separate.
- TV Senado has specific commercial/political-use restrictions; NATV permissions depend on the individual work and verified KOGL marking. Suriname requires written permission for multimedia reproduction.
- Montserrat footer terms/privacy links point to an unrelated template demonstration; they do not establish parliamentary reuse terms.
