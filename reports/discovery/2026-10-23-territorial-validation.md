Cook Islands’ Constitution reprinted 13 June 2022 establishes lawmaking,
responsible government, appropriations and expenditure scrutiny (articles 13,
39, 70–71). NZ MFAT confirms self-government in free association: national
classification with CK map coverage is appropriate; New Zealand is a search
association, not a provincial parent. The constitution is a retained reprint,
not a claim that no subsequent amendments exist.

Guernsey’s official lawmaking, budget and scrutiny descriptions establish scope.
It is a Crown Dependency jurisdiction, not part of the UK. The regional outline
selects the Guernsey main-island polygon from the coarser Natural Earth GGY
aggregate, whose source label misleadingly says Sark. The GG country overview
retains all three source components; the documented regional outline excludes
Sark and Alderney. Generalisation omits small outlying islets. This does not claim
those separate legislatures or their proceedings. Wikidata Q1368324 retains the
older gov.gg/parliament URL, which returned a security block; current parliament.gg
identifies the States and links its dated proceedings. No block was bypassed.

Bangsamoro geography is narrowed using PSA’s summary and official notices:
five provinces, three cities and 105 municipalities, including the SGA’s eight
municipalities/63 barangays. Sulu is assigned to Region IX under EO91 of 30 July
2025. Do not add all of Cotabato Province or use the old ARMM outline. The newer
World Bank boundary catalogue is CC BY 4.0 and has metadata dated 21 October
2026, but its actual BARMM/SGA geometry is not yet inspected. HDX returned 403.
The August 2025 BAA77 district allocation was struck down; the official January
2026 committee report says BAA58 is not revived. Neither is a current district map.
Signed RA12317 clarifies holdover and the 30 October assumption date. Homepage
headlines and recurring calendar entries do not establish actual sittings or
current elected membership.

Bangsamoro’s detailed terms conflict with its generic public-domain footer:
retain restrictions and the separate hyperlink-notification/permission blocker.
Any proposed outreach must be prepared for user review before sending. Vojvodina
connection failures describe this environment, not worldwide availability.

All useful publication surfaces are reconciled for both additions: catalogue,
localized jurisdiction names, local flags, UK/NZ search associations, map and
planned calendars. Totals: **129 sources** (51 national, 72 regional/territorial,
six supranational), **53 mapped regional jurisdictions**, 75 link-outs, 35 formal
candidates (31 promoted, three researching, one rejected). These are local changes,
not a deployment claim. No stable HLS, IPTV addition or implemented XMLTV parser.
