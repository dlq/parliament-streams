## India — india-state-legislatures (28 bodies)

Decision: **eligible_legislature**. The Supreme Court of India explains that state legislatures enact statutes on State List and Concurrent List subjects. These 28 named state assemblies qualify as components of state legislatures; a legislative council, where present, is another chamber and requires its own service checks. Union territories are assessed under their separate legal bases.

This settles the constitutional scope gate, not current assembly membership, adjournment, dissolution or President’s Rule. Confirm current institutional activity before describing a recording as a current/live sitting, particularly Manipur. Municipal councils remain outside scope.

- https://www.sci.gov.in/constitution/

## India — india-delhi-assembly (1 bodies)

Decision: **eligible_legislature**. Article239AA establishes Delhi’s Legislative Assembly and substantive State/Concurrent List lawmaking with reserved-subject exceptions. Official Assembly material confirms budget and collective ministerial responsibility/no-confidence functions. Delhi is a national-capital union territory with a legislature, not an ordinary municipal council.

Reserved subjects and subsequent central legislation constrain its powers; this is not full statehood or a claim of legislative supremacy.

## Malaysia — malaysia-state-legislatures (13 bodies)

Decision: **eligible_legislature**. The current federal MyGovernment institutional description identifies Dewan Undangan Negeri as elected state lawmaking assemblies. ## Spain — spain-autonomous-community-parliaments (17 bodies)

Decision: **eligible_legislature**. The Constitution and current Ministry of Territorial Policy explain autonomous-community self-government through their statutes and legislative assemblies. The ministry’s comparative statutory parliament chapter covers all17 communities’ legislation, budget and government control. They qualify as autonomous legislatures; Ceuta and Melilla have distinct statutes and are not included in this decision.

The ministry labels its comparative table as statutes current in March2011. Use it for the structural lawmaking distinction, with the current institutional page and Constitution; it is not a certification of every later statutory amendment, current membership or broadcast service.

## Switzerland — swiss-cantonal-parliaments (22 bodies)

Decision: **eligible_legislature**. The Federal Chancellery’s current CHInfo explanation identifies a parliament, government and constitution for each of the26 cantons, with cantonal responsibility and taxation. These are constituent-canton parliaments, not communal councils. Cantonal lawmaking is subject to popular votes; Appenzell Innerrhoden and Glarus retain distinct Landsgemeinde arrangements, assessed separately below.

Framework-level scope classification only, not an individual audit of all current cantonal constitutional versions, parliament rooms or media rights. Cantonal institutions and communal parliaments must remain distinct.

- https://www.ch-info.swiss/en/die-schweiz/foederalismus

## Switzerland — glarus-landrat-landsgemeinde (1 bodies)

Decision: **eligible_legislature**. Current official cantonal constitutional text Articles82/89 identifies the Landrat as parliament and supreme supervisory body: it prepares Landsgemeinde legislation, enacts authorized ordinances, and can legislate urgently pending the next Landsgemeinde. Article90 gives budget/accounts powers. The Landsgemeinde itself enacts constitutional/statutory law. This is a cantonal legislative system, not a municipal assembly.

Do not present the representative Landrat as the sole final lawmaker; distinguish Landrat and Landsgemeinde events and require actual proceedings/source validation.

- https://gesetze.gl.ch/app/de/texts_of_law/I%20A%2F1%2F1
- https://www.landsgemeinde.gl.ch/topics/allgemeine-informationen

## Switzerland — appenzell-innerrhoden-grosser-rat-landsgemeinde (1 bodies)

Decision: **eligible_legislature**. The canton’s official explanation distinguishes the Landsgemeinde, the GrosserRat and the executive Standeskommission. The cantonal parliament prepares constitutional/statutory proposals for the Landsgemeinde, enacts implementing ordinances and resolutions, supervises all authorities and determines budget, tax rate and accounts. It qualifies as part of the cantonal parliamentary/legislative system, not an ordinary local council.

Final primary lawmaking is shared with the Landsgemeinde, so do not imply autonomous final Act-making by the GrosserRat alone. Verify exact event identity before any addition.

- https://www.ai.ch/politik
- https://www.ch-info.swiss/en/die-schweiz/foederalismus

## France — france-ordinary-mainland-regional-councils (12 bodies)

Decision: **outside_default_scope**. Constitution Article72 grants regulatory power for assigned competencies, and CGCT L4221-1 limits regional deliberations to powers allocated by national law. The Interior Ministry describes their development, transport/education and coordination role and removal of the general-competence clause. These12 ordinary mainland regional councils are outside the catalogue’s substantive autonomous-lawmaking scope; elected membership, budget votes and service regulations alone are insufficient.

This is the project scope interpretation of ordinary statutory regional powers, not a claim of no regulatory force. It does not decide Corsica, Article73/74 overseas habilitations, New Caledonia/provinces or French Polynesia; those need their individual current arrangements.

## Spain — ceuta-melilla-special-municipal-autonomy (2 bodies)

Decision: **outside_default_scope**. Their current autonomy statutes Articles12/13/21 distinguish budget, scrutiny, proposals to national lawmakers and regulatory norm-making from autonomous-community legislative power. The Constitutional Court’s STC240/2006 expressly rejects legislative power for Ceuta and locates such legislation at state level. These special autonomous city assemblies remain outside this catalogue scope; regulatory sanctions and the right to propose a national law do not make them autonomous lawmaking parliaments.

Retain special-city status rather than labelling them autonomous communities. Older draft statutes promising legislative powers and litigants’ arguments in judgments are not enacted law or the court’s holding. Reopen on an enacted change to powers.

## Australia — australian-indian-ocean-shire-councils (2 bodies)

Decision: **outside_default_scope**. The Australian National Audit Office’s current external-territory service-delivery audit identifies Christmas Island and Cocos(Keeling) shires as local councils providing specific local-government functions such as waste services. WA’s current planning explanation expressly identifies the Christmas shire as local government. Their elected councils and local laws do not constitute territorial self-government legislatures.

Do not call the islands ordinary parts of Western Australia. Commonwealth territorial responsibility, applied WA law and service arrangements are distinct from a self-governing ACT/NT-style legislature.
