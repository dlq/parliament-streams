# Panama collective-land and people-wide congress legal holds — 24 October 2026

The four cases remain **specific authority evidence holds**. This pass confirms legally consequential customary governance and narrows the exact documents needed. It does not establish eligibility or justify treating these bodies as merely cultural/local organizations.

The user’s boundary remains first-level and autonomous special-status legislatures. Nesting, ethnic membership, small population and unwritten custom do not decide eligibility. Territorial general rules and congress-level financial/executive powers must be established.

## What the law confirms

[Law 72/2008](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/wp_repo/blogs.dir/cendoj/PUEBLOS_INDIGENAS/ley_72_de_2008_que_establece_el_procedimiento_especial_para_la_adjudicacion_de_la_propiedad_colectiva_de_tierra_de_los_pueblos_indigenas_que_no_estan.pdf), Articles 13–15, protects coordination/consent and provides executive recognition of traditional organization and authorities. Its titling framework must not be reduced to a claim that customary governments have no powers. [Law 467/2025](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2025/07/665/ley-467-de-2025.pdf), Articles 77–79, recognizes traditional justice in collective lands, requires compliance with traditional-authority decisions subject to competent review, and guarantees resources. It supersedes the 2016 law. Neither provision by itself identifies each named General Congress as the holder of general legislative and budget-approval powers.

The 2025 CONDIPI decree separately names these four bodies. National development representation is confirmed, while each body’s territorial competence needs its own governing instrument.

## Congreso General Tierras Colectivas Emberá y Wounaan

`panama--embera-and-wounaan-collective-lands` — retained `specific_authority_evidence_hold`.

A distinct multi-community General Congress outside the statutory Emberá-Wounaan comarca, with regional congresses documented in its own account. The 2023 Tuira regulation belongs to a regional formation within this network; its scope cannot be silently expanded to all five historical regions or the General Congress.

The General Congress and local/community decision structures are documented separately from the comarca. The Tuira regulation process covers justice, authority certification, resource rights and outsider restrictions; generic law recognizes collective-land customary justice.

Still required:

- Current adopted governing statute/Carta Orgánica or authenticated customary-law record of the General Congress, with territorial annex and adoption/amendment record.
- Full Reglamento Interno de la Región del Tuira validated in May 2023 and its final adoption/operative record, identifying powers reserved to the General Congress.
- The applicable executive recognition/coordination instrument under Law 72 Article 15, if issued, and congress resolutions or customary rules establishing budget and executive controls.

Primary evidence: [law 72](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/wp_repo/blogs.dir/cendoj/PUEBLOS_INDIGENAS/ley_72_de_2008_que_establece_el_procedimiento_especial_para_la_adjudicacion_de_la_propiedad_colectiva_de_tierra_de_los_pueblos_indigenas_que_no_estan.pdf), [decree 223](https://www.anati.gob.pa/normativas/), [law 467](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2025/07/665/ley-467-de-2025.pdf), [law 467_status](https://infojuridica.procuraduria-admon.gob.pa/norma_screen.php?numsec=64619), [condipi 2025](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/30204/GacetaNo_30204_20250124.pdf), [ministry 2019](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Analisis-Sociocultural-PN-L1157-marzo-2019-.pdf), [tuira 2023](https://www.fscindigenousfoundation.org/es/tag/cartas-organicas/), [collective_own 2009](https://www.forestcarbonpartnership.org/sites/fcp/files/Documents/PDF/Jul2010/III%20Informe_Final_REDD_Tierras_Colectivas_ED.pdf), [three_congresses 2024](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/inicia-proceso-de-consultas-con-los-pueblos-indigenas/).

## Congreso General Emberá Alto Bayano

`panama--embera-alto-bayano` — retained `specific_authority_evidence_hold`.

General Congress for the Alto Bayano Emberá collective-land communities in eastern Panamá, distinct from both the Darién collective-lands congress and Madungandí. Primary accounts identify Ipetí, Piriatí and Majé/Unión communities, but contemporary exact membership requires its governing record. Ordinary district location is not by itself disqualifying.

Its own organizational account places local congresses and Council of Nokos below the General Congress. Government agreements and 2024 meetings separately recognize the cacique and congress leadership, and collective-land/customary justice law preserves consequential community authority.

Still required:

- Current adopted Reglamento/Estatuto del Congreso General Emberá de Alto Bayano or authenticated governing customary instrument, with adoption record and community/territorial coverage.
- The applicable executive recognition/coordination instrument under Law 72 Article 15, if issued; precise general norm-making clauses and enforceable congress resolutions.
- Congress-level financial rules/approved accounts and appointment, removal or reporting rules governing the cacique and administration.

Primary evidence: [law 72](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/wp_repo/blogs.dir/cendoj/PUEBLOS_INDIGENAS/ley_72_de_2008_que_establece_el_procedimiento_especial_para_la_adjudicacion_de_la_propiedad_colectiva_de_tierra_de_los_pueblos_indigenas_que_no_estan.pdf), [decree 223](https://www.anati.gob.pa/normativas/), [law 467](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2025/07/665/ley-467-de-2025.pdf), [law 467_status](https://infojuridica.procuraduria-admon.gob.pa/norma_screen.php?numsec=64619), [condipi 2025](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/30204/GacetaNo_30204_20250124.pdf), [ministry 2019](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Analisis-Sociocultural-PN-L1157-marzo-2019-.pdf), [alto_own 2009](https://www.forestcarbonpartnership.org/sites/fcp/files/Documents/PDF/Jul2010/VIII_Informe_REDD_Embera_Alto_Bayano_.pdf), [alto_agreement 2015](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/ministro-henriquez-firma-acuerdo-con-miembros-del-congreso-de-alto-bayano/), [three_congresses 2024](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/inicia-proceso-de-consultas-con-los-pueblos-indigenas/).

## Congreso Nacional Pueblo Wounaan

`panama--wounaan-people` — retained `specific_authority_evidence_hold`.

People-wide Wounaan congress with members inside the Emberá-Wounaan comarca and other congress territories, plus its own collective-land communities outside them. This is not simply a third chamber of the comarca, nor merely an NGO: WPNP/CNPW and the sibling FUNDEPW must remain distinct. The name National does not establish a national parliamentary chamber.

Primary indigenous and government accounts document plenary approvals, its own collective-land communities and a Puerto Lara land-use authorization. Law 467 recognizes consequential traditional justice. Members’ existing comarca jurisdiction and nationwide affiliation overlap; the 2010 account explicitly distinguishes them.

Still required:

- Full adopted Carta Orgánica del Congreso Nacional del Pueblo Wounaan/Wounaan Podpa Nʌm Pömaam from the 2022–23 process, its adoption/amendment/operative record and territorial/competence annex.
- Applicable traditional-organization recognition instrument under Law 72 Article 15, if issued, and rules separating CNPW territorial powers from comarca and other congress powers.
- Financial statute and plenary resolutions establishing who approves/audits resources and controls the cacique/executive, with FUNDEPW’s separate role.

Primary evidence: [law 72](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/wp_repo/blogs.dir/cendoj/PUEBLOS_INDIGENAS/ley_72_de_2008_que_establece_el_procedimiento_especial_para_la_adjudicacion_de_la_propiedad_colectiva_de_tierra_de_los_pueblos_indigenas_que_no_estan.pdf), [decree 223](https://www.anati.gob.pa/normativas/), [law 467](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2025/07/665/ley-467-de-2025.pdf), [law 467_status](https://infojuridica.procuraduria-admon.gob.pa/norma_screen.php?numsec=64619), [condipi 2025](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/30204/GacetaNo_30204_20250124.pdf), [ministry 2019](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Analisis-Sociocultural-PN-L1157-marzo-2019-.pdf), [wounaan_charter 2023](https://www.fscindigenousfoundation.org/es/tag/carta-organica/), [wounaan_own 2009](https://www.forestcarbonpartnership.org/sites/fcp/files/Documents/PDF/Jul2010/V%20Info%20final%20REDD_Congreso_Wounaan.pdf), [wounaan_minas 2026](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/PMAS-PUERTO-LARA.pdf), [wounaan_own 2021](https://www.mdpi.com/2313-5778/5/4/91).

## Consejo General Pueblo Bribri

`panama--bribri-people` — retained `specific_authority_evidence_hold`.

Council and Bulú of the Panamanian Bribri ancestral territory in Bocas del Toro, not the Costa Rican Bribri development associations. The ministry describes clan-based consensus; 2025 participant evidence names El Guabo,Namüwökir, Dakle and Bris. Do not make territorial title or cross-border jurisdiction claims from ethnic continuity.

Government identifies the Council and Bulú as distinct offices; fieldwork records clan-consensus decisions. Participatory 2025 plans include land-use/conservation rules and strengthen the Council. Generic law respects traditional authority, but its specific allocation cannot be inferred from a title application or development-plan report.

Still required:

- Current governing customary instrument or authenticated record of Consejo General Bribri/Bulú competencies, including territorial/community reach and adoption/recognition history.
- Full Plan de Desarrollo Comunitario and Plan de Conservación Comunitario 2025–2035 adopted 13 July 2025, with assembly minutes, maps, binding-rule and accountability provisions.
- Any applicable executive recognition/coordination instrument under Law 72 Article 15, plus Council financial and executive-accountability rules; obtain full current court/title records separately before claiming settled delimitation.

Primary evidence: [law 72](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/wp_repo/blogs.dir/cendoj/PUEBLOS_INDIGENAS/ley_72_de_2008_que_establece_el_procedimiento_especial_para_la_adjudicacion_de_la_propiedad_colectiva_de_tierra_de_los_pueblos_indigenas_que_no_estan.pdf), [decree 223](https://www.anati.gob.pa/normativas/), [law 467](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2025/07/665/ley-467-de-2025.pdf), [law 467_status](https://infojuridica.procuraduria-admon.gob.pa/norma_screen.php?numsec=64619), [condipi 2025](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/30204/GacetaNo_30204_20250124.pdf), [ministry 2019](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Analisis-Sociocultural-PN-L1157-marzo-2019-.pdf), [bribri_ministry 2017](https://www.mingob.gob.pa/en-mingob-se-reunen-con-autoridades-del-territorio-bribri/), [bribri_plans 2025](https://www.acicafoc.org/2025/07/14/comunidades-bribri-de-panama/), [bribri_court_lead 2026](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/storage/gacetas/2026/08/30597_B/GacetaNo_30597b_20260825.pdf).

## Evidence and access limits

The complete 232-page MinGob 2019 report was downloaded; the governance discussion at pp 42–49 was read. The 2025 hosting date does not make those findings current fieldwork. The 2019 report’s note 77 contains an apparent comarca-name error; that sentence is not used as operative jurisdiction evidence.

The 2023 Tuira account describes a **regional** internal-regulation process. It cannot establish the General Congress’s complete current powers. The separate Naso article mentions the Wounaan charter process; its 180 articles and Naso validation date do not belong to Wounaan.

Puerto Lara’s 2026-hosted PMAS reports Congress land authorization and includes a 2022 consultation; the signed scanned authorization was not independently read. Bribri’s 2025 plans were reported adopted by their implementing partner, but full plan texts were not obtained.

An [official 2026 Gazette Bribri judgment](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/storage/gacetas/2026/08/30597_B/GacetaNo_30597b_20260825.pdf) is a useful title/delimitation lead; only indexed passages were available. Its full date, dispositif and all grounds were not certified, and it is not used to deny customary authority.

COONAPIP’s own 2009–10 accounts clarify historical institutional relationships; their exact present-day community counts and membership are not certified. Direct FCPF access returned 403. Older and newer sources give different Wounaan founding years; that discrepancy does not decide scope.

All four reviews are complete as a bounded public-primary-source pass. Four operative-instrument holds remain. No metadata, stream, EPG, rights, catalogue or shared-register edits were made. The [JSON report](2026-10-24-legal-holds-panama-collective.json) records source-by-source access scope, confirmed powers, exact gaps and reopening instruments.
