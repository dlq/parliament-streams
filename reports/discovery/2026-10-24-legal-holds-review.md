## What changed

- **France:** Guadeloupe’s former energy grant has an explicit 27 June 2021 expiry. The parliamentary review records the unfulfilled renewal request. Guyane and Mayotte have an ordinary combined departmental/regional framework plus conditional authorization procedures. The 2026 Martinique law applies to Martinique. These three receive dated project exclusions with precise reopening tests; this does not deny their real regulatory/budget powers or guarantee no later grant could exist. Two independent reviews checked this application of the scope rule. [French findings](2026-10-24-legal-holds-france.md).
- **Panama:** Law 72 article 15 recognizes traditional organization/authorities; Law 467/2025 recognizes binding customary-justice decisions and resources in collective lands. Executive Decree 4/2025 separately identifies the Buglé, Tagarkunyala and Naso bodies. Recognition, customary justice, territorial representation and an operative congress-level lawmaking/finance allocation remain separate questions. [Collective-land cases](2026-10-24-legal-holds-panama-collective.md) · [Naso/Buglé/Tagarkunyala](2026-10-24-legal-holds-panama-territorial.md).
- **FNTA:** the full 30 September Gazette was recovered and its government/Assembly sections OCR-checked. The official bulletin confirms unanimous passage of the amended Bill on 3 September 2026. Assent, final Act number/text, commencement and constitution remain unverified. Incomplete NeVA/Governor/Gazette registers cannot establish nonexistence or noncommencement. ## Exact remaining instruments

The eight held entries stay outside the authority-cleared service-validation queue. Their public/customary status is not rejected simply because a state decree or webpage is unavailable. A complete currently applied customary governing text or authenticated authority allocation may resolve a case even where state sanction is not the source of its powers.

### Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority (FNTA)

- Assented final Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority Act, 2026, or an authenticated official publication identifying its Act number and assent date together with the final text.
- Official Gazette commencement notification under the final Act, including any different dates for individual provisions, and the constitution/establishment order if separately required.

### Consejo General Pueblo Bribri

- Current governing customary instrument or authenticated record of Consejo General Bribri/Bulú competencies, including territorial/community reach and adoption/recognition history.
- Full Plan de Desarrollo Comunitario and Plan de Conservación Comunitario 2025–2035 adopted 13 July 2025, with assembly minutes, maps, binding-rule and accountability provisions.
- Any applicable executive recognition/coordination instrument under Law 72 Article 15, plus Council financial and executive-accountability rules; obtain full current court/title records separately before claiming settled delimitation.

### Congreso General Buglé

- Obtain the approved Congreso del Pueblo Buglé internal/customary regulation, adoption minutes or resolutions and effective-date evidence, including any final successor to the proposal described by the COONAPIP forum report.
- Establish whether that instrument creates autonomous special-status territorial jurisdiction rather than only people-wide representation, cultural consultation or an internal association.
- Identify the operative provisions for general binding norms, territorial revenue/funds/budget accountability, and executive appointment/removal/reporting; show their allocation relative to the joint comarca congress to avoid duplicate publication.

### Congreso General Emberá Alto Bayano

- Current adopted Reglamento/Estatuto del Congreso General Emberá de Alto Bayano or authenticated governing customary instrument, with adoption record and community/territorial coverage.
- The applicable executive recognition/coordination instrument under Law 72 Article 15, if issued; precise general norm-making clauses and enforceable congress resolutions.
- Congress-level financial rules/approved accounts and appointment, removal or reporting rules governing the cacique and administration.

### Congreso General Tierras Colectivas Emberá y Wounaan

- Current adopted governing statute/Carta Orgánica or authenticated customary-law record of the General Congress, with territorial annex and adoption/amendment record.
- Full Reglamento Interno de la Región del Tuira validated in May 2023 and its final adoption/operative record, identifying powers reserved to the General Congress.
- The applicable executive recognition/coordination instrument under Law 72 Article 15, if issued, and congress resolutions or customary rules establishing budget and executive controls.

### Congreso General Guna Dagargunyala

- Obtain an authentic Congress-specific customary governing instrument or adopted general resolutions showing the Congress’s jurisdiction and their binding territorial effect, adoption/currentness and review/enforcement process.
- Recover provisions or documented exercised powers for finance/budget/fund accountability and appointment/removal/reporting of territorial authorities.
- Distinguish ancestral regional government from the Púcuro/Paya local assemblies; do not import Gunayala Fundamental Law, its budgets or cross-region cultural declarations without an express applicable grant. A national state Act is not required if operative customary authority can be proved.

### Consejo General Pueblo Naso Tjer-di

- Recover the complete Council-validated 180-article Charter and its adoption resolution; establish whether and when it became operative as customary territorial law.
- Locate any executive decree sanctioning that Charter and its Gazette number, promulgation and commencement; if it was not sanctioned, do not infer that customary authority cannot nevertheless exist.
- Read the actual allocation between Council, King and people’s assembly, including binding territorial norms, funds/budgets and executive accountability. If the assembly is the legislative organ, correct the institution identity rather than clearing the municipal council.

### Congreso Nacional Pueblo Wounaan

- Full adopted Carta Orgánica del Congreso Nacional del Pueblo Wounaan/Wounaan Podpa Nʌm Pömaam from the 2022–23 process, its adoption/amendment/operative record and territorial/competence annex.
- Applicable traditional-organization recognition instrument under Law 72 Article 15, if issued, and rules separating CNPW territorial powers from comarca and other congress powers.
- Financial statute and plenary resolutions establishing who approves/audits resources and controls the cacique/executive, with FUNDEPW’s separate role.
