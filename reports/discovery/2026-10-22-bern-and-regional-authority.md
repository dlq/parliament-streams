## Catalogue interpretation

A first-level representative assembly with autonomous power to enact generally binding regional norms affecting rights or obligations, together with budget and executive-scrutiny powers, can qualify. Sovereignty, a federal constitution and the English label Act are not required. Municipalities remain outside the geographic scope, even where national law gives them similar ordinance powers. This does not make every regional council eligible.

This is the project’s eligibility judgement based on the cited powers, not a claim that every regional council is a parliament.

## Nagasaki Prefectural Assembly

**Eligible under the catalogue scope rule.** The prefectural assembly exercises substantive autonomous ordinance-making, budget and executive-scrutiny powers. Its ordinances can impose obligations and restrict rights and can provide penalties within statutory limits. This goes beyond meeting administration or a specific delegated service rule. The same statutory framework also covers municipalities; those remain outside the agreed geographic scope.

First-level prefecture of Japan; not a sovereign or federal state.

Official evidence:

  Articles: 2: prefectural/municipal functions and autonomous versus statutory entrusted functions; 14: ordinances, rights/obligations and penalties; 15: executive regulations distinguished from assembly ordinances; 96: assembly approval of ordinances, budget and accounts; 100: investigation powers; 178: no-confidence and dissolution mechanism.
- [Opened official assembly explanation.](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/gikai/4020.html). Institution-specific confirmation of ordinance enactment, budget approval, important appointments and executive investigation.

Resolve Nagasaki eligibility positively and retain its published official committee links. Other prefectures can use the shared statutory analysis, but their individual source ownership, institutional identity and broadcast validation remain separate.

## Twelve Dutch provincial councils

**Eligible under the catalogue scope rule.** The councils have autonomous provincial ordinance-making powers, initiative and amendment rights, budget authority and executive accountability/inquiry powers. Binding rules can apply directly to residents and businesses. National-law limits and the term verordening do not negate legislative authority. Municipal councils also have ordinance powers but remain outside the geographic scope.

Provinces of the European Netherlands; separate from the Kingdom's constituent countries and Caribbean public bodies.

Resolve the twelve province ledger rows positively on the shared legal framework. This is not validation or promotion of twelve broadcast services and does not decide island-council eligibility.
