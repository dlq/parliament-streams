# National Chamber Inventory — 24 October 2026

> Snapshot note: this report precedes the final scope decisions and operational integration. Use the [current reconciliation](2026-10-24-inventory-reconciliation.md) and [institution register](../../data/discovery/institutions.json) for the applied decisions and remaining legal holds.

Reconstructed **103 named main national chambers across all 71 baseline countries**: 102 from the IPU Parline directory and Taiwan separately from its own legislature. This is 32 bicameral and 39 unicameral country groups in the source classification.

**This does not establish a complete national/subnational census or current broadcast coverage.** IPU lists Indonesia as DPR-only/unicameral; the separately identified DPD and MPR need explicit constitutional-role/relationship reconciliation, not omission or automatic extra-chamber counting. Source translations (such as Japan’s upper chamber being called Senate) are preserved as provider labels, not imposed as preferred institutional names.

The earlier full API response exceeded the local 25 MB resource limit; a public field-selected export returned 315,073 bytes. The raw export hash, retrieval date, provider notes/status fields and request URL are retained. No login or access workaround was used.
