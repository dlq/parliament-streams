# Flag Asset Attribution

- `bern.svg` is the [canton of Bern flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Canton_of_Bern.svg),
  Paul Boesch's official design, marked public domain as a Swiss public-law
  body's coat of arms. The original SVG is unchanged; copyright status does
  not remove the separate rules governing official insignia.

- `je.svg`, `im.svg`, `pf.svg`, `nc.svg`, and `sx.svg` use flag-icons v7.3.2
  under its MIT license, like the national flags below. The New Caledonia file
  is the Kanak flag used by that library; its display does not decide status.

- `cordoba.svg` is the [2014 flag of Córdoba Province, Argentina](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bandera_de_la_Provincia_de_C%C3%B3rdoba_2014.svg),
  designed by Cristian Baquero Lazcano, with SVG revisions by RapiarteBanderas,
  Hurfer and HapHaxion; licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  The downloaded SVG is unchanged.
- `nagasaki.svg` is the [Nagasaki Prefecture flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Nagasaki_Prefecture.svg),
  vector by Alex Tora, public domain under Article 13 of Japan's Copyright Act.
- `santa-catarina.svg` is the [Santa Catarina state flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bandeira_de_Santa_Catarina.svg),
  vector by OAlexander, recorded as public domain in Brazil on its source page.
- `at.svg`, `jp.svg`, `mu.svg`, and `ch.svg` use the same flag-icons v7.3.2
  MIT-licensed source as the other national flags below.

- `quebec.svg`, `ontario.svg`, and `nunavut.svg` are public-domain flag files
  downloaded from Wikimedia Commons:
  [Quebec](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Quebec.svg),
  [Ontario](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Ontario.svg), and
  [Nunavut](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Nunavut.svg).
- `osce.svg` is the flat [OSCE flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Organization_for_Security_and_Co-operation_in_Europe.svg),
  based on the organization's official flag specifications.
- The Canadian sub-national flag files are public-domain assets from Wikimedia
  Commons: [British Columbia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_British_Columbia.svg),
  [Alberta](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Alberta.svg),
  [Saskatchewan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Saskatchewan.svg),
  [Manitoba](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Manitoba.svg),
  [Prince Edward Island](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Prince_Edward_Island.svg),
  [Northwest Territories](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Northwest_Territories.svg),
  and [Newfoundland and Labrador](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Newfoundland_and_Labrador.svg).
- The Australian state flag files are public-domain assets from Wikimedia
  Commons: [New South Wales](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_New_South_Wales.svg),
  [Victoria](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Victoria_(Australia).svg),
  [Queensland](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Queensland.svg),
  and [Western Australia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Western_Australia.svg).
- The German Land flag files are public-domain assets from Wikimedia Commons:
  [North Rhine-Westphalia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_North_Rhine-Westphalia.svg),
  [Baden-Wurttemberg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Baden-W%C3%BCrttemberg.svg),
  and [Bavaria](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Bavaria_(lozengy).svg).
- The Spanish autonomous-community and Jalisco flag files are public-domain
  assets from Wikimedia Commons: [Catalonia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Catalonia.svg),
  [Valencia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Valencian_Community.svg),
  [Andalusia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Andalusia.svg),
  [Navarre](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Navarre.svg), and
  [Jalisco](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Jalisco.svg).
- The United States state flag files are public-domain assets obtained from the
  [US state flags SVG collection](https://github.com/nibsbin/us-state-flags-svg),
  which records Wikimedia/Wikipedia public-domain provenance: California,
  Florida, Minnesota, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Texas, Utah, and
  Washington.
- The remaining national, devolved-jurisdiction, European Union, and United
  Nations flag SVGs are from
  [flag-icons v7.3.2](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/v7.3.2), under
  the MIT licence reproduced in `LICENSE-flag-icons.txt`.

## Dutch provincial flags

Bundled from Wikimedia Commons on 22 October 2026; the file pages identify these
Dutch provincial flag depictions as public domain.

- `drenthe.svg`: [Flag of Drenthe.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Drenthe.svg), GeeKaa, later SVG optimization by HapHaxion; public-domain release.
- `limburg.svg`: [Flag of Limburg (Netherlands).svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Limburg_(Netherlands).svg), Louis Maris design, SVG by GeeKaa and D’Arch; public-domain Dutch provincial flag.
- `noord-brabant.svg`: [North Brabant-Flag.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:North_Brabant-Flag.svg), GeeKaa and later optimizations; public-domain release.
