# What belongs in Parliament Streams

The catalogue is for legislatures with **legislative authority**: national
parliaments and their chambers, and state, provincial, territorial, autonomous
or devolved legislatures. Public committee proceedings of an eligible
legislature belong in scope too.

This working rule reflects the project decision of 22 October 2026. The wider
discovery inventory deliberately searched more broadly; inclusion in that
research ledger is not a decision to publish a body in the catalogue.

## Assess authority, not the name

For a new institution, retain an official constitutional, statutory or
institutional source establishing:

1. its position as a legislature in the relevant system of government;
2. its substantive law-making powers within its jurisdiction, and its budget
   and executive-scrutiny role;
3. its territorial and constitutional relationship, including any autonomy,
   free association or disputed status.

An elected membership, a televised meeting, parliamentary-style procedure,
budget approval or delegated local by-law powers alone are insufficient.
Ordinary city/town councils, intermunicipal planning councils, administrative
coordination bodies and service-delivery boards are outside the catalogue.

Names do not decide eligibility. A Swiss cantonal parliament or the Isle of
Man's Legislative Council can qualify despite the word “council”. Conversely,
an institution called an “assembly” is not automatically a parliament.
Territorial legislatures need not have sovereign or unlimited powers.

Autonomous regional legislation can be called an **ordinance** rather than an
Act. For a first-level assembly, a general power to enact binding regional
norms affecting rights or obligations, backed by budget and executive-scrutiny
powers, can satisfy this rule. Check the extent and legal basis of that power;
a narrow delegated service regulation alone remains insufficient. The ordinary
municipal exclusion still applies where municipalities have similar powers.

## Borderline and special cases

Keep a documented authority review before promoting regional assemblies whose
powers may resemble local government, including Nordic regional councils,
Japanese prefectural assemblies, Dutch provincial bodies and comparable
county/departmental assemblies. Do not infer a common answer from an English
translation of their names. Existing entries are reviewed individually rather
than silently removed.

The [22 October authority resolution](../reports/discovery/2026-10-22-bern-and-regional-authority.md)
confirms Nagasaki Prefectural Assembly and the twelve Dutch provincial councils
as eligible on this basis. Nagasaki's autonomous ordinances and Dutch provincial
ordinances are substantive regional law-making despite national-law limits.
This settles their authority gate; each broadcast service still needs its own
identity, access and source-conditions review. It does not decide eligibility
for Dutch Caribbean island councils or automatically publish other Japanese
prefectures. Skåne remains outside scope under its separately documented
regional service/local-government assessment.

Indigenous traditional congresses, special-territory councils and capital
district bodies need individual legal-authority assessment. An exception is
not established simply by geographic remoteness or special administrative
status. Appointed executives and administrations are not legislatures.

Free-associated states and constituent countries must retain their correct
relationships; they are not ordinary provinces of the audit's grouping
country. Disputed institutions require neutral status evidence. Existing
international institutional coverage is separately labelled supranational;
it does not create a general council-inclusion rule.

## Publication and other surfaces

Only an eligible institution with a validated official viewing service is
promoted. Preserve the evidence for source ownership, chamber/committee
identity, access, source conditions and accessibility. An archive or audio
service may be useful when accurately described.

Every promotion must be reconciled across catalogue search, jurisdiction
relationships, the coverage map and schedule source coverage. A viewing page
does not establish stable HLS, and a meeting calendar does not establish an
implemented XMLTV parser. IPTV and EPG exports retain those separate gates.

The broad research ledger retains excluded and unresolved bodies so future
research does not repeat the same searches. Its counts describe discovery
work, not the number of eligible parliaments or usable streams.
