### What each result means

A **check** is an observation, such as a successful page load or advancing decoded frames. **Scoped validation** combines primary identity/provenance and relevant checks to support a named claim, such as recorded proceedings on a particular date. Current live, every room, language/audio tracks, source-wide accessibility and media reuse require their own evidence. An attempted check with no confirmation stays unresolved; it is not a pass or proof of no service. A publication hold identifies a specific condition preventing publication, rather than merely unfinished research.

The evidence register does not label all652 bodies fully validated. Already-confirmed publication identity/recordings are retained, so future work can address the remaining claims without repeatedly restarting them.
